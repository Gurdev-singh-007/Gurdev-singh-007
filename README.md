### 🕹️ The Real Foundation (Before Code)
Long before I wrote my first line of code or knew what programming meant, I was already deep inside hardware. Growing up in a family-run PlayStation gaming center, my early exposure was hands-on:

* **Hardware & Teardowns:** Diagnosing motherboard issues and fixing PS1, PS2, and PS3 controllers.
* **Pure Gameplay Muscle Memory:** Beating classic action-adventure titles on **Hard Difficulty** (Crash Bandicoot, Prince of Persia, Pitfall, Prototype, Sly Cooper).

> *This gave me a natural, low-level intuition for how game hardware behaves under load and how tight gameplay feels to the player.*
